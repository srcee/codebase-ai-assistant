# Refund approval

A refund moves through four states. Each change is written to the audit log with the person who made it.

## States

1. Requested: a Support Agent or Reviewer opened the request.
2. Under review: a Reviewer or the Owner picked it up.
3. Approved or Rejected: the decision, with a reason for a rejection.
4. Paid out: the payment provider confirmed the money was returned.

## Service levels

A refund request must get a decision within two business days. Requests older than two business days are flagged in red on the Refunds page, and the Owner receives a daily digest of flagged requests.

## Escalation

Refunds above 500 EUR skip the Reviewer queue and go straight to the Owner. If the Owner is away, nobody else can approve them; the request stays in Under review until the Owner returns.

## Rejected refunds

A rejection needs a reason that the customer can read. The Support Agent who opened the request is notified and may open a new request with more evidence.

# Product publishing

## Drafts

Every product change starts as a draft. A draft can be edited by Owners, Admins, and Reviewers, and it is invisible to customers until it is published.

## Review and publish

A Reviewer or Admin opens the draft, checks the photos, price, and stock level, and selects Publish. The four-eyes rule applies: the person who edited the draft last cannot publish it.

## Scheduled publishing

A draft can be scheduled to go live at a future date and time in the store's time zone. Scheduled drafts are locked against edits; unschedule a draft to change it again.

## Rolling back

Published products keep their last ten versions. Restore an earlier version from the History tab; the restored version becomes a new draft that must be published again.

# Staff onboarding

## Invitations

An Owner or Admin invites a new staff member by email from the Team page and picks their role. The invitation link expires after 72 hours; an expired invitation can be sent again.

## First sign-in

On first sign-in the new staff member sets a passphrase and turns on two-factor authentication. The Back Office does not open any page until both steps are done.

## Offboarding

When someone leaves, an Admin deactivates the account instead of deleting it, so the audit log keeps their name. Deactivated accounts can no longer sign in, and their open refund requests return to the Reviewer queue.

# Audit log

## What is recorded

The audit log records sign-ins, role changes, refund decisions, product publications, price changes, and payout detail changes. Each entry has the time, the staff member, the action, and the affected record.

## Retention

Entries are kept for 400 days and then deleted automatically. Entries cannot be edited or deleted by anyone before that, including the Owner.

## Exporting

Owners, Admins, and Auditors can export the audit log as a CSV file for a chosen date range. An export is itself written to the audit log.

## Auditor access

An Owner grants the Auditor role for a fixed period of up to 30 days. When the period ends the account is deactivated automatically.

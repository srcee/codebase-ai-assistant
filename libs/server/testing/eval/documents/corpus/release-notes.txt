Harbor Shop Back Office release notes

Release 4.2
Refund requests older than two business days are now flagged in red, and the Owner receives a daily digest of flagged requests.
The Auditor role can export the audit log as a CSV file.

Release 4.1
Scheduled publishing lets Reviewers set the date and time at which a product draft goes live.
Products keep their last ten versions; earlier versions can be restored from the History tab.

Release 4.0
Hardware security keys are now required for Owners and Admins.
Invitation links expire after 72 hours instead of seven days.

# Roles

Harbor Shop Back Office gives every staff account exactly one role. A role decides which screens a person sees and which actions they may confirm. Owners and Admins assign roles on the Team page.

## Choosing a role

Pick the role with the fewest permissions that still lets the person do their job. Give temporary access for an external audit with the Auditor role instead of a full account; it expires on its own.

## Role list

| Role | Purpose | Typical holder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Full control, including billing and closing the store | Store founder |
| Admin | Manages staff, store settings, and integrations | Operations lead |
| Reviewer | Approves refunds and catalog changes made by others | Team lead |
| Support Agent | Answers customers and opens refund requests | Customer support |
| Viewer | Reads orders and reports without changing anything | Finance analyst |
| Auditor | Reads the audit log for a limited period | External auditor |

## Changing a role

A role change takes effect at the next sign-in. The person keeps their order history and notes, but drafts they cannot edit under the new role become read-only for them.

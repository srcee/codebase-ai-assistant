# Reviewer

## Responsibilities

A Reviewer is the second pair of eyes for money and catalog changes. Reviewers approve or reject refund requests opened by Support Agents, and they approve product drafts before they go live.

## Four-eyes rule

Nobody may approve their own work. A Reviewer cannot approve a refund request they opened themselves, and cannot publish a product draft they edited last. The Back Office hides the Approve button in both cases.

## Limits

A Reviewer may approve refunds up to 500 EUR per order. Larger refunds are routed to the Owner automatically. Reviewers cannot cancel orders, archive products, or change prices in bulk.

## Becoming a Reviewer

An Admin promotes a Support Agent or a Viewer to Reviewer on the Team page. The new Reviewer must have two-factor authentication turned on before the role is saved.

# Frequently asked questions

## Can I create a custom role?

No. Harbor Shop has six fixed roles. If a role gives someone too much access, pick a role with fewer permissions; if it gives too little, ask an Admin to change it.

## Why can't I see the Approve button?

Either your role cannot approve the item, or the four-eyes rule applies because you opened or last edited it yourself.

## Who can close the store?

Only the Owner. Closing the store stops all sales immediately and starts a 30 day period in which the Owner can still reopen it.

## How do I change the store currency?

The store currency is chosen when the store is created and cannot be changed later. Prices are shown to customers in their local currency using the daily exchange rate.
